Professionalism-Reflection





**Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?**

&#x09;I have and what made it effective is having the same goal and having the drive to accomplish them. Being able to incorporate yourself to people with the same objectives as you just makes the work a 100% better as you could easily understand each other and you would always have a healthy debates about the solutions the team thought of.



**What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?**

&#x09;What we could do is to be open for other's suggestions, construct a proper plan, deliver it as concisely as possible and ask the team questions or let them point out some concerns to device solutions to further bullet proof the plan.



**How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?**

&#x09;To do this, we should always keep professionalism as the number 1 thing on our daily agenda. We should not let anyone feel small for having different solutions or perspective than us and always receive anyone with respect and don't tolerate disrespectful behavior.



**How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?**

&#x09;Organizing yourself. It could be as simple as creating notes to be discussed or just receiving everyone with a smile. We should also practice not bringing other matters in a professional environment especially if it is completely unrelated to what we are supposed to do.



**What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?**

&#x09;I think it would look like always participating on standups and asking questions. As an intern myself, it would not be surprising to anyone to say that you don't know something. As intern we are still new to this things and clearly still figuring things out for ourselves. So being proactive in this internship would be asking questions, and a lot of it to be exact.



**How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?**

&#x09;Typically you should talk directly to the person in question but in times where they can't be reached or unavailable, what I would do is I will ask the person in charge of them. I would state that I've tried reaching out to this person due to this reason and still willing to wait on a given time but if still they didn't reach out to me regarding the matter, I would kindly ask the supervisor for some supervision on the matter. Of course this varies on the urgency but it is still crucial that we do not bypass anyone's authority on proceeding in such matters.



**What are some things you won't do?**

&#x09;What I won't do is probably doing things on my own and bypassing other's authority.
