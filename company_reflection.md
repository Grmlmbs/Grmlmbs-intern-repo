**Why do you think Focus Bear was created?**



&#x09;Based on what I've read in the website, Focus bear was created to help support people with ADHD in daily tasks and time management. These people tend to have short attention spans and have trouble focusing on one task actively and they constantly seek fulfillment or stimulus to continue on something.



**What problem is Focus Bear solving?**



&#x09;Its trying to solve the lack of focus of people with ADHD or similar condition. They also aim to supplement their well being by helping them have a more structured and stricter lifestyle to help improve their focus and self-management.



**Why do you think this mission is important?**



&#x09;I think this mission is important because more and more people are developing this condition this days due to how social media and how the environment moves this days. Everyone pursues efficiency and finishing things as much as possible. This shift in phase on how we do things makes our brains adapt through limiting focus on one thing so it could work on the next thing it sees which is a problem and a poison for productivity. That is why managing oneself and a task one at a time is crucial to prevent any burnout and messy work. That's why this mission is important.
