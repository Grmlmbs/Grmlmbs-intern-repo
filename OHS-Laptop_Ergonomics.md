**What equipment changes can you make to improve your workspace setup? (e.g., using an external monitor, adjusting your chair, using a laptop stand)**

&#x09;To improve workspace setup, it is crucial that we make adjustments that will help us work with a proper natural posture as to not strain our body as we do a lot of things in long periods of time. One thing we could do is to use attachments such as stands for our laptop and using external keyboards and mouse. We could also use a proper office chair that helps support our back and wearing glasses that would help protect our eyes.



**What behavioural changes can you implement to improve posture and reduce strain? (e.g., sitting upright, taking regular breaks, adjusting screen height)**

&#x09;To improve posture and reduce strain, it is very essential that we take breaks. It helps improve our posture and helps with keeping focus to the task as well.
