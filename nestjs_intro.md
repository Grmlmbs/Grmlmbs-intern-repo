#### **6.1 What is NestJS? (Framework Overview)**



**What are the key differences between NestJS and Express.js?**



&#x09;NestJS has a strict modular architecture and has a built in dependencies but it is highly scalable that's why it is used for big projects and deployments and has a steeper learning curve. On the other hand, Express.js is more free on the structure, you can install dependencies on your own and your free to design it how you want giving it a much more manageable learning curve.



**Why does NestJS use decorators extensively?**

&#x09;

&#x09;It is a has much cleaner structure and removes the need for manually connecting routes, services, middleware and guards.

&#x09;

**How does NestJS handle dependency injection?**



&#x09;NestJS automatically injects the dependencies you need and takes care of scanning classes, registering injectables, resolve dependencies automatically and injects them into constructors.



**What benefits does modular architecture provide in a large-scale app?**



&#x09;Having a modular architecture makes the apps functions separate from one another, scalable, Easier for teams to work on and collaborate with, Makes things reusable, provides better maintainability and clear dependency boundaries.
