Cyber-Security-Guidelines



**What security measures do you currently follow, and where can you improve?**



I'm currently using fingerprint verification with my phone and I also use 2 factor authentication on my accounts. But I think I could improve it by setting up and creating a recovery accounts that has a robust security.



**How can you make secure behavior a habit rather than an afterthought?**



Maybe creating a schedule or plans on when you should update your passwords and security details. Make reminders in a calendar app to keep you notified and you can stay reminded to make changes to your account passwords.



**What steps will you take to ensure your passwords and accounts are secure?**



We should follow formatting requirements and creating a password with special characters that only us would know or better yet always create a second form of verification when logging in to your account and always update your recovery credentials as well to make sure that you can recover your account whenever there were mishaps or you happen to get hacked.



**What would you do if you suspected a security breach or suspicious activity on you account?**



You should always review such issues first because there were times that it was just you but you used a unrecognized device, nowadays accounts still flags such events because it could still be a security breach and you might be unaware of it. If you don't recognized such activity you can report it and change your credentials or better yet to actually protect your sensitive information, you could try encrypting you credentials if available.
