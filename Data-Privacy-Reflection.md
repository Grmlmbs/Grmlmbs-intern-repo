Data-Privacy-Reflection



**What steps can you take to ensure you handle data securely in your daily tasks?**

&#x09;When it comes to handling data securely, I make sure to know how important a data is because there were data that can't really be deleted right away and there were data that you need to hold on to for some time before actually deleting them. That's why I always create backups and always set a transaction with proper measures to handle data properly.



**How should you store, share, and dispose of sensitive information safely?**

&#x09;When storing data, it should always be encrypted especially if it is sensitive and can't be shared to just anyone. Typically we don't want to share data willingly that is why we require validation checks and credentials are demanded to ensure that only the intended user can access their data. When it comes to disposing data, we always have to follow standards on how long should we hold on to data.



**What are some common mistakes that lead to data privacy issues, and how can they be avoided?**

&#x09;Some common mistakes that users typically do is sharing their credentials to other people and setting up weak credentials which makes their data more vulnerable to cyberattacks and hackers alike. To solve this, many organizations implements a 2 factor authentication and validation to ensure data safety.



One habit that I do is I always ensure that my recovery credentials for my accounts is always updated nowadays.
