##### **How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?**



Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment by checking on progress of each team member. It brings every individuals thought together and helps everyone updated of their progress. It also helps team leaders evaluate what to do next and what to prioritize to do in certain situations.
