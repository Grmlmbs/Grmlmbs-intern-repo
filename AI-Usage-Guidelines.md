AI-Usage-Guidelines



**When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?**

In my opinion, you should use AI to assist you in thinking and aid you with coming up with solutions from problems. Like a study buddy.



**How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?**

I do believe that using it to supplement your thoughts is not a bad idea, you should ask it questions to deeply know and understand a problem, break down things for you to make it simpler to digest for you. That way you can gain more insight about something and device a proper plan that you can guarantee gives you less risks.



**What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?**

You typically don't share sensitive details about your life with AI so you could protect yourself but either way I think AI already have policies for that. Regardless, you should apply copyright licenses on intellectual properties and always protect and think about what you share with AI.
