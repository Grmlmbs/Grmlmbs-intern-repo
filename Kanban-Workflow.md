Kanban-Workflow





**How does Kanban help manage priorities and avoid overload?**



&#x09;Kanban visualizes all work. It tells you what tasks exist currently, what is currently active, what is blocked, and what has already been completed to help teams visualize what their supposed to do and to avoid any misestimation of work. In Kanban, works usually follow hierarchy(highest to lowest priority) this manages priorities properly and makes assignments more organized. It also has a system which limits just how many task should be active at a time which is called Work-In-Progress(WIP) which helps avoid burnout and piling up of unfinished tasks. Also, to avoid further overload, it tracks progress rather than activity which gives a sense of reward right away. Lastly, one good thing about Kanban is that it exposes bottlenecks early so teams can focus resources in that appropriately.



**How can you improve your workflow using Kanban principles?**



&#x09;Kanban can help improve workflow by incorporating it to your whole system as a basis on how your gonna do tasks. Using Kanban, you can create simple board that tracks your progress, Limit you active tasks so you wouldn't be overloaded by unnecessary tasks at the moment, breakdown large tasks, review and track your progress and gives you a clear track on what to do next making overall work flowy rather than jumping from one activity to the next.
