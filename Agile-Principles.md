Agile-Principles



**What do you think are the biggest benefits and challenges of Agile?**



&#x09;In my opinion, the biggest benefit of agile development is its adaptability while still delivering what it's expected to deliver because of it's iterative and continuous nature. On the other hand, I think it becomes a double edged blade of some sort. It makes managing changes difficult because you have to actually minimize chaos and risks as well as you go through the continuous changes which can be tricky to manage at times.



**How can Agile principles help improve your role and responsibilities?**



&#x09;Agile principles can help you improve role and responsibility by changing how you think about work, manage tasks, and collaborate with others. It helps you prioritize delivering value rather than just finishing tasks, Break down work into smaller, manageable tasks, Improve continuously through feedback, Improve time management and Be more adaptable when requirements change, Learn to self-manage and build better mindset, and improve communication and teamwork on a team.
