Inclusive-Design-Reflection



**Who are considered vulnerable populations, and what challenges might they face in digital spaces?**

&#x09;Typically, people with diagnosed disabilities are vulnerable in digital space especially if they have trouble focusing, seeing or expressing themselves properly in digital spaces. Some challenges they might face is having trouble focusing on some tasks, difficulties on getting things done on time, and getting exposed to bullying online.



**What ethical considerations are important when designing or working with neurodivergent individuals? (Hint: Avoid overwhelming UX, respect sensory needs, ensure clear communication.)**

&#x09;Avoiding designs that overwhelms the user is very essential. We should always consider how such user would interact to our app and guide them to achieve the desired goal that they need to help them use the app properly. We should avoid words that might come off as judgmental and make things as predictable as possible.



**How can you make interactions and content more accessible for people with ADHD or Autism? (e.g., simple language, predictable navigation, reducing cognitive load)**

&#x09;We should be clear and direct, Reduce information overload, avoid judgmental language, Make communication predictable, Allow different communication styles.



**How can we support neurodivergent team members in a professional setting? (Hint: Be clear in communication, respect different working styles, and allow flexibility.)**

&#x09;We should have and practice considerations to them and let them work in a way that works for them as long as it helps them get productive results at work and give them a more flexible environment for them so they can easily navigate and regulate themselves in the workplace.
